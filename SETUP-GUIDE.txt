FileSaathi - setup guide (GitHub + Google Drive sync)
=====================================================

Total time: about 15 minutes, one time only. Everything here is free.
Replace USERNAME below with your GitHub username.


A. PUT THE APP ON GITHUB (5 minutes)
------------------------------------
1. Go to https://github.com and sign in (or create a free account).
2. Top right "+" > "New repository".
     Repository name:  filesaathi
     Visibility:       Public   (needed for free GitHub Pages; only app code
                                 goes here, never your documents)
   Press "Create repository".
3. On the new page, click the link "uploading an existing file".
4. Unzip FileSaathi-App.zip on your computer. Open the "FileSaathi" folder,
   select EVERYTHING inside it (index.html, config.js, sw.js,
   manifest.webmanifest, the icons folder, the .txt files) and drag it onto
   the GitHub page. Wait for all files to show, then press
   "Commit changes".
   Check: the repository must show index.html at the top level, not inside
   another folder.
5. Open the repository's "Settings" tab > "Pages" (left menu).
     Source: Deploy from a branch
     Branch: main   Folder: / (root)   > Save
6. Wait 1-2 minutes and refresh. Your app link appears:
     https://USERNAME.github.io/filesaathi/
   Open it to check it works.


B. CREATE THE GOOGLE CLIENT ID (8 minutes)
------------------------------------------
This tells Google that your FileSaathi is allowed to ask for access to a
FileSaathi folder in your Drive. Google's screens change name sometimes;
look for the same words.

1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Google account
   whose Drive you want to use.
2. At the top, open the project list > "New project". Name: FileSaathi >
   Create. Make sure the new project is selected at the top.
3. Search bar at the top: type "Google Drive API" > open it > "Enable".
4. Left menu (or search): "Google Auth Platform" / "OAuth consent screen" >
   "Get started":
     App name:            FileSaathi
     User support email:  your Gmail
     Audience:            External
     Contact email:       your Gmail
   Agree and press "Create".
5. Open "Audience" > "Test users" > "Add users" > add your Gmail address,
   and the Gmail of anyone in your family who will use the same app on
   their phone > Save.
   (FileSaathi only asks for the "drive.file" permission, the narrowest
   Drive permission Google has.)
6. Open "Clients" (or "Credentials" > "Create credentials" >
   "OAuth client ID"):
     Application type:               Web application
     Name:                           FileSaathi
     Authorized JavaScript origins:  https://USERNAME.github.io
                                     (exactly this: https, no "/filesaathi",
                                     no slash at the end)
   Press "Create".
7. Copy the "Client ID". It ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com
   (You do NOT need the "Client secret". Ignore it.)


C. CONNECT THE APP TO DRIVE (2 minutes)
---------------------------------------
1. On GitHub, open your filesaathi repository > click config.js > pencil
   icon (Edit).
2. Paste the Client ID between the quotes:
     googleClientId: "1234567890-abc123.apps.googleusercontent.com"
   Press "Commit changes". Wait 1 minute for GitHub Pages to update.
3. Open https://USERNAME.github.io/filesaathi/ > press "Connect Google
   Drive" at the top > choose your Google account > Continue.
   Google may show "Google hasn't verified this app": this is normal for
   your own private app. Press "Continue".
4. Done. A "FileSaathi" folder appears in your Google Drive, with your files
   sorted as Master folder / Sub-folder / Year / file.


D. INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE AND OTHER DEVICES
------------------------------------------
Open https://USERNAME.github.io/filesaathi/ on each device:
  - Android (Chrome):  "Install app" button at the top, or menu > Install.
  - iPhone (Safari):   Share button > "Add to Home Screen".
  - Computer (Chrome/Edge): install icon in the address bar.
Then press "Connect Google Drive" once on that device. The same files appear
everywhere and stay in sync.

Sign-in lasts about an hour at a time. When the top button says
"Reconnect Drive", tap it; it takes a second and no password is needed.


E. AI READING AND RENAMING (optional)
-------------------------------------
Settings > Document reading > paste a Claude API key from
https://console.anthropic.com (usage is billed by Anthropic). Enter it on
each device. Never put this key in config.js or anywhere on GitHub.


F. GOOD TO KNOW
---------------
- Your documents go only to your device and your Google Drive. GitHub only
  serves the app.
- Do not rename or delete the file "FileSaathi-index.json" or the
  "_app-data" folder in Drive: they hold your names, folders and dates.
- Deleting a file in FileSaathi (from the recycle bin) moves it to Google
  Drive's Trash, where Google keeps it for 30 days.
- To update the app later, upload the new files to the same repository
  ("Add file" > "Upload files"). Every device picks up the new version.
- Problems? "This web address is not allowed": check step B6 (origin must
  be exactly https://USERNAME.github.io). "Access blocked / not a test
  user": add that Gmail in step B5.
